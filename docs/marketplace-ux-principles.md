# Marketplace UX Principles

## Core Rule

Users came for a map. Marketplace features must feel like useful local context, not a feed pasted on top of a map.

## Entry Points

- Visitor mode: attractions, hawker food, cafes, hotels, events.
- Local mode: HDB nearby services, daily deals, classes, community posts.
- Driver mode: parking deals, workshops, fuel, EV charging, moving services.
- Transit mode: station-area food, events, last-mile offers.

## Surfaces

### Map Search

When the user searches a place, show:

- Official places first.
- Relevant offers around that place.
- Marketplace listings only after the location result is clear.

### Place Detail

Place detail can show a small contextual strip:

- Food nearby.
- Current offers.
- Rooms nearby.
- Moving-out sales nearby.
- Events this week.

No infinite feed in map detail.

### Marketplace Tab

The tab is for intentional browsing:

- Search.
- Category filters.
- Map/list toggle.
- Distance and freshness filters.
- Saved and contacted listings.

### Posting

Posting must be short:

1. Choose category.
2. Add title, price, images.
3. Pin location or choose existing place.
4. AI suggests cleaner text and category.
5. Submit for review.

## Category Design

### Eat And Play

Best for visitor mode and local weekend usage.

- Hawker highlights.
- Food deals.
- Attractions.
- Events.
- Family activities.

### Rental

High value, high risk. Needs stricter verification.

- Require precise area but allow approximate address until contact.
- Require report button.
- Add scam warnings.
- Agent/owner label.
- No deposits through LokaLane at launch.

### Second-Hand

Useful around moving, student, expat and neighbourhood use cases.

- Location radius.
- Pickup method.
- Condition.
- Image quality.
- Expiry date.

### Supply And Demand

Demand posts can create liquidity before supply grows.

- Looking for room.
- Looking for furniture.
- Looking for service provider.
- Looking for travel buddy/carpool interest later only with strong safety controls.

## Paid Placement Rules

- Always label paid content.
- Do not show paid cards during active navigation.
- Limit paid cards per viewport.
- Use relevance first, payment second.
- Allow users to hide irrelevant sponsored content.

## AI UX

AI should assist, not take over:

- Suggest better listing title.
- Translate listing.
- Summarize long description.
- Explain nearby transport.
- Warn about suspicious listing patterns.

Never present AI output as official data unless it comes from official providers.
