LokaLane Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2026

LokaLane is a free local map and mobility app for Singapore and Malaysia. This policy explains the privacy approach for the early public version of the app.

Information We Use

LokaLane may use your device location to center the map, show nearby places, and improve commute context. The MVP is designed not to store precise location history on our servers.

Reports And Corrections

If you submit a place correction, incident report, or feedback item, the app may process the details you provide so the report can be reviewed, grouped with similar reports, and improved over time.

Official And Open Data

LokaLane may use official and open data sources such as OneMap, LTA DataMall, and OpenStreetMap. Provider credentials are kept server-side and are not shipped inside the client app.

Advertising

LokaLane may show clearly labelled sponsored content or native ads in non-navigation surfaces such as search results or place details. Ads are not intended to cover the main map or interrupt active navigation. If a future native build uses advertising identifiers, the store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Analytics

The early MVP should keep analytics minimal. If analytics are added, they should be used to understand app reliability and feature usage, not to sell personal location history.

Children

LokaLane is not designed for children as a primary audience.
